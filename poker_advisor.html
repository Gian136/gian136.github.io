The odds assume opponents hold random cards. The recommendation comes from a reinforcement-learning model: a Q-table trained on 32 million simulated hands against copies of itself and loose rule-based players, which taught it what bets of different sizes usually mean. It sees your hand strength, the betting round, the price to call, your stack relative to the pot and how many opponents are left – not position, earlier betting or who the opponents are. The expected values are the model's estimates from those training games. A guide, not a guarantee.
